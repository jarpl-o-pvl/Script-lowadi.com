A highly advanced, fully automated, and safe Tampermonkey script designed to manage, train, and grow your horses on Lowadi/Howrse. Featuring a sleek, modern UI, smart DOM interaction, and a comprehensive safety system to ensure your horses never die.

✨ Key Features
🎨 Modern Glassmorphism UI: A sleek, draggable, dark-themed control panel that doesn't obstruct your gameplay.

🧠 Smart Age Progression: Automatically detects the horse's age (from a few hours up to 1 year and 4 months) and executes a highly customized care routine for each specific stage.

🏠 Auto-KSK (Riding Center): Automatically registers your horse in the best available riding center (Forest, 10 days) if they are not already in one.

🎯 Precision Slider Targeting: Accurately clicks on custom data-number DOM elements to set the exact amount of feed, oats, training hours, and playtime.

🏖️ Beach & Trail Walks: Automates forest and mountain walks, and intelligently selects the exact skills to improve and worsen on the beach.

🔄 Anti-Freeze & Auto-Resume: If the page reloads or changes (e.g., moving to the next horse), the bot automatically resumes its work using a "Watchdog" timer.

🍪 Auto-Accept Cookies: Automatically detects and clicks cookie consent banners (OneTrust) so they never block the bot.

🛡️ Safety Mechanisms (Anti-Death System)
We know how frustrating it is to lose a horse. This script is built with strict safety protocols:

💀 Critical Health Stop: If the horse's health drops below 20%, the bot instantly stops all operations and alerts you in the log.

⚡ Energy Guard: The bot will automatically skip training if the horse's energy is below 40%, preventing overwork.

⚠️ Dynamic Feeding: If the game displays a warning like "Your horse is too skinny, give 20 fodder," the bot reads this number and automatically adjusts the slider to 20 to save the horse's weight.

🚀 Installation
Make sure you have the Tampermonkey extension installed in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Opera).

Click on the Tampermonkey icon and select "Create a new script".

Delete all the default code in the editor.

Copy the entire script code from this repository and paste it into the editor.

Press Ctrl + S (or Cmd + S on Mac) to save.

Refresh your Lowadi/Howrse page. The bot panel will appear in the top-right corner.

🕹️ How to Use
Modes: Choose between 💪 Кач (Training) for full care and training, or 🏃 Прогон (Run) to quickly progress through multiple horses.

Settings (⚙ Настройки):

Set your preferred amounts for Feed, Oats, and Walk hours.

Configure the specific skill and hours for Training (e.g., Trot, 3 hours).

Set the Beach skills (+ / -).

Start: Click ▶ Старт (Start). The bot will begin analyzing the page and performing actions.

Monitor: Keep an eye on the 📋 Лог (Log) tab to see exactly what the bot is doing in real-time.

🧬 Age-Based Automation Logic
The bot automatically applies the following routines based on the horse's age:

Hours - 4 Months: Milk, Groom, Sleep, Grow.

6 Months: Auto-KSK, Feed (6kg), Groom, Sleep, Grow.

8 Months: Feed (6kg), Play (8h), Pet, Water, Carrot, Groom, Train, Sleep, Grow.

10 Months: Feed (6kg), Play (10h), Pet, Water, Carrot, Groom, Train, Sleep, Grow.

1 Year: Feed (8kg), Play (10h), Pet, Water, Carrot, Groom, Train, Sleep, Grow.

1 Year 2 Months: Feed (8kg), Play (10h + 2h), Pet, Water, Carrot, Groom, Train, Sleep, Grow.

1 Year 4 Months: Feed (8kg), Play (8h), Pet, Water, Carrot, Groom, Train, Sleep, Grow.
